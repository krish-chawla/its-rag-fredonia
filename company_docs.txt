Q: What types of flowers do you carry?
A: We carry a wide selection including roses, lilies, sunflowers, tulips, orchids, carnations, and seasonal wildflowers. Availability may vary depending on the season. Call us or check in-store for current stock.

Q: Do you offer wedding flower packages?
A: Yes, we offer wedding packages including bridal bouquets, bridesmaid bouquets, boutonnieres, centerpieces, and ceremony arrangements. Wedding orders require a consultation at least 2 weeks in advance and a 50% deposit to reserve your date.

Q: Can I order flowers for a funeral or memorial service?
A: Yes, we specialize in sympathy arrangements including wreaths, sprays, and standing arrangements. We recommend placing funeral orders at least 24 hours in advance. Same-day sympathy arrangements may be available — please call us directly to confirm.

Q: Do you have ready-made bouquets available for walk-in customers?
A: Yes, we keep a selection of pre-made bouquets available daily for walk-in purchase. Selection varies by day. No advance order is needed for these.

Q: What is your pricing range for arrangements?
A: Our arrangements range from $25 for small bouquets to $200 or more for large custom arrangements. Wedding and event packages are priced separately based on consultation. Delivery fees are $10 for all orders within our 15 mile radius.

Q: Can I place an order online or by phone?
A: At this time we do not have an online ordering system. Orders can be placed by calling us directly or visiting the store in person. Phone orders for same-day delivery must be placed before 2pm.

Q: What happens if the flowers I ordered are not available?
A: If a specific flower you requested is unavailable, we will contact you before fulfilling the order to discuss a suitable substitute. We will never substitute without your approval.

Q: How should I care for my flowers after purchase?
A: To extend the life of your flowers, trim the stems at an angle, place them in clean water with the provided flower food packet, keep them away from direct sunlight and heat, and change the water every two days. Most arrangements last 5 to 7 days with proper care.

Q: Do you offer flower subscriptions or recurring delivery?
A: Yes, we offer weekly and bi-weekly flower subscription plans starting at $40 per delivery. Subscribers receive a seasonal arrangement of our choosing. Contact us in-store or by phone to set up a subscription.

Q: Can I include a card or personal message with my order?
A: Yes, we include a complimentary card with any delivery order. You can provide your message when placing the order by phone or in person.

Q: What if my flowers arrive damaged or in poor condition?
A: If your delivery arrives damaged, contact us within 24 hours and we will arrange a replacement or issue store credit. We stand behind the quality of every arrangement we deliver.

Q: Do you offer discounts for bulk or corporate orders?
A: Yes, we offer discounts for bulk orders of 10 or more arrangements and for corporate accounts placing recurring orders. Contact us directly to discuss pricing and set up a corporate account.

Q: Are your flowers locally sourced?
A: We source from a mix of local growers in the Western New York region when available, and from trusted national suppliers for flowers not grown locally. We prioritize freshness in all sourcing decisions.

Q: Do you offer plant delivery in addition to cut flowers?
A: Yes, we carry a selection of potted plants including succulents, peace lilies, and seasonal plants. Potted plants are available for both in-store pickup and delivery within our standard delivery radius.

Q: Do you deliver outside of Fredonia or to surrounding areas?
A: Yes, we deliver to surrounding areas within a 15 mile radius of our store. This includes Fredonia, Dunkirk, and nearby towns. A $10 delivery fee applies to all delivery orders. We do not deliver beyond the 15 mile radius.

=== RETURNS, REFUNDS, AND CANCELLATIONS ===
Q: What is your return policy?
A: Because fresh flowers are perishable, we cannot accept returns of fresh flowers or arrangements once they have been picked up or delivered. Instead, we offer a Freshness Guarantee: if your flowers do not stay fresh for at least 5 days with proper care, contact us and we will replace the arrangement or give store credit. Please contact us within 7 days of delivery or pickup and include a photo if possible.

Q: Can I return a plant or a non-flower item?
A: Potted plants and gift items (vases, plush animals, chocolates in their sealed box) can be returned unused and in original condition within 7 days with your receipt, for a refund to the original payment method. Plants must be returned in healthy condition.

Q: What if my order arrives damaged or wrong?
A: If your delivery arrives damaged, wilted, or is not what you ordered, contact us within 24 hours of delivery. We will send a replacement at no charge or issue a full refund, whichever you prefer. A photo of the arrangement helps us resolve it quickly.

Q: Can I cancel or change my order?
A: Yes. Regular orders can be cancelled for a full refund up to 24 hours before the scheduled delivery or pickup time. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before delivery receive store credit instead of a refund, because the flowers have usually already been prepared. Same-day orders cannot be cancelled once the arrangement has been made. Changes to the message card, delivery address, or delivery time are free if requested at least 4 hours before delivery.

Q: What is the cancellation policy for weddings and events?
A: The 50% wedding and event deposit is non-refundable within 30 days of the event date, because flowers are ordered from growers in advance. Cancellations more than 30 days before the event receive a full refund of the deposit minus a $50 consultation fee. The remaining balance is due 7 days before the event.

Q: How long do refunds take?
A: Refunds go back to the original payment method and usually appear within 5 to 7 business days. Store credit is issued immediately and never expires.

Q: What if the recipient is not home for the delivery?
A: If no one is home, our driver will leave the flowers in a safe, shaded spot (if available) and leave a card, or call the recipient to arrange redelivery. Redelivery on the same day is free; redelivery on a different day costs the standard $10 delivery fee. If the address you gave us was incorrect, a new delivery fee applies.

=== FLOWER SAFETY ===
Q: Are any of your flowers dangerous for pets?
A: Yes. Lilies (including Easter lilies and stargazer lilies) are highly toxic to cats, even in small amounts such as pollen or vase water. If you have cats, tell us when ordering and we will make a lily-free arrangement. Roses, sunflowers, orchids, and most succulents are generally considered pet-friendly.

Q: Do you have options for people with allergies?
A: We can build low-pollen arrangements using flowers like roses, orchids, tulips, and hydrangeas, and remove pollen-heavy stamens from lilies on request. Just tell us about allergies when ordering.
